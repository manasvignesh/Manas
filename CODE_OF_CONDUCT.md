# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and consider how simulations can affect real people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report concerns privately to the project maintainers. Enforcement should be fair, proportionate, and focused on restoring a safe collaborative environment.
